# CMSV6 Digital PR and Link-Earning Plan

## Principles

- Earn links with useful original evidence and tools.
- Do not automate spam outreach, purchase bulk links, use fake authors or offer undisclosed followed-link incentives.
- Personalise outreach around a publication’s real audience and editorial policy.

## Ethical outreach groups

- Fleet-management and video-telematics publications
- Haulage, HGV and logistics publications
- Taxi and private-hire trade organisations
- Farming and agricultural publications
- Agricultural suppliers and rural connectivity specialists
- CCTV and vehicle-electronics installers
- Marine-equipment and vessel-operations websites
- Business-security and multi-site operations publications
- UK technology directories and relevant local business organisations
- Existing iCustodian suppliers, manufacturers and partners

## Outreach workflow

1. Finish and technically review the asset.
2. Identify ten to twenty genuinely relevant publications or organisations.
3. Read each target’s recent coverage and submission policy.
4. Send a short, individual note explaining the reader value and original evidence.
5. Disclose iCustodian ownership and commercial interest.
6. Track replies, earned links, referral traffic and qualified enquiries.
7. Update the asset when external feedback identifies a real gap.

## Quality measures

- Relevant referring domains, not raw link count
- Editorial links to the most useful page
- Referral visits that engage with guides, compatibility or pricing
- Mentions from recognised industry organisations
- No manual-action or spam-pattern warnings
