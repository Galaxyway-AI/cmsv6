# CMSV6 SEO Measurement Plan

## Primary outcomes

Prioritise qualified impressions, relevant clicks, compatibility requests, enquiries, device activations, hosting selections and compatible-equipment referrals. Average ranking position alone is not a success measure.

## Baseline and ongoing metrics

| Metric | Source | Baseline timing | Review cadence | Decision use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Indexed and excluded pages | Google Search Console/Bing | After verification | Weekly first month; monthly thereafter | Find indexing/canonical problems |
| Organic impressions, clicks and CTR | Search Console/Bing | First complete 28 days | Monthly | Improve pages with relevant impressions and weak clicks |
| Query position distribution | Search Console | First 28 days | Monthly | Strengthen pages in positions 4–20 |
| Branded vs non-branded queries | Search Console/Bing | First 28 days | Monthly | Measure discovery beyond CMSV6 brand |
| Enquiry and compatibility conversions | Consent-aware analytics and email records | After analytics connection | Monthly | Evaluate qualified commercial performance |
| Pricing-plan selections | Consent-aware analytics | After analytics connection | Monthly | Understand term interest without treating clicks as purchases |
| iCustodian referrals | UTM reporting on iCustodian | After cross-domain analytics access | Monthly | Measure equipment journey |
| Core Web Vitals | Search Console field data | When sufficient traffic exists | Monthly | Prioritise real-user performance |
| Crawl errors and sitemap status | Search Console/Bing | After verification | Weekly first month | Fix discovery and status problems |
| Backlinks and referring domains | Search Console/Bing plus approved backlink tool | Initial export | Quarterly | Measure earned authority and relevance |

## Reporting groups

- CMSV6 brand
- Live CCTV and remote viewing
- GPS tracking
- Fleet and vehicles
- Taxi and private hire
- HGV and haulage
- Farm and livestock
- Business premises
- Marine
- Pricing, compatibility and activation
- Support and troubleshooting

## 30/60/90-day review

- 30 days: validate indexing, canonical selection, crawl errors and first queries.
- 60 days: improve titles/intros on relevant pages receiving impressions but weak CTR; strengthen internal links.
- 90 days: consolidate weak overlap, expand only proven clusters, begin approved ethical outreach and compare qualified conversions with the first complete month.

## Data safeguards

Never place CCTV footage, GPS coordinates, passwords, device IDs, complete support messages or full form contents in analytics. Use aggregate route, event and non-sensitive category fields only after the visitor accepts analytics.
