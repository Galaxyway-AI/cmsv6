## Publication governance

- Review Search Console impressions before adding overlapping pages.
- Keep one primary page per commercial intent.
- Consolidate rather than multiplying weak variants.
- Add genuine photographs, diagrams, test data and case studies only with permission and evidence.
- Do not fabricate local pages, regulatory approval or product support.
